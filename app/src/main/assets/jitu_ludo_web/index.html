📜 Ludo Rules

  • Roll 6 to bring a token out of the base yard.
  • Rolling 6 gives an extra turn!
  • Land on an opponent's token on regular squares to capture it back to their base & earn an extra roll!
  • Tokens on Star squares are safe from capture.
  • Tokens require an exact roll to enter the center Goal.
  • First player to bring all 4 tokens Home wins!
